# 📊 Customer Churn Diagnosis & Business Intelligence Dashboard

An end-to-end **Business Intelligence** project that analyzes customer churn patterns, quantifies revenue loss, and identifies the key drivers behind customer attrition using **SQL, Excel, and Power BI**.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Customer churn directly impacts revenue and long-term business growth. This project transforms raw customer transaction and support data into an interactive Power BI dashboard that helps stakeholders:

- Measure customer churn and retention
- Quantify revenue loss caused by churn
- Identify the major churn drivers
- Compare customer behavior across sales channels
- Detect high-risk customer segments and cities
- Support data-driven retention strategies

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **SQL** – Data cleaning, transformation & analysis
- **Microsoft Excel** – Data preprocessing
- **Power BI** – Interactive dashboard development

# 📈 Key Insights

- **94.37%** overall customer churn rate
- Over **₹5 Billion** in revenue identified as at risk
- Jaipur recorded the highest revenue exposure
- Customer support issues were a major churn driver
- Revenue loss remained consistently high across multiple years
- Significant variation in churn across sales channels
- High-value customers contributed substantially to revenue loss

---

# 💡 Business Recommendations

- Improve customer support processes
- Launch targeted retention campaigns in high-risk cities
- Strengthen loyalty programs for high-value customers
- Optimize underperforming acquisition channels
- Continuously monitor churn KPIs through executive dashboards
